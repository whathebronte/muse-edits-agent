# The independent reviewer and rubric

Instructions for the reviewer pass in the practice system. The reviewer is a separate agent session from the builder. It never touches the editing app. It judges the plan, the script, preview frames and the export, cold.

## How the review runs

1. The builder finishes v1 and hands over: the plan, the script (if any), preview frames, the export, and the set's slot (benchmark, fresh or experiment).
2. The reviewer scores v1 against the rubric below and the creator's style rules, and writes specific catches: what is wrong, where (timestamp or frame), and what would fix it. Vague praise or vague criticism is a failed review.
3. The builder fixes the catches into v2.
4. The reviewer re-scores v2, blind to its own v1 comments where practical.
5. Only v2 is presented to the creator, with its score. If v2 still scores under the pass mark, it is presented anyway, with the score and the reviewer's top reason. Hiding weak work teaches the system nothing.
6. The creator's verdict outranks the score. Every disagreement between the creator and the reviewer is logged in the feedback file, and the rubric is recalibrated toward the creator.

## The rubric (100 points)

Quality, 90 points:

| Criterion | Points | What earns them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hook in the first 3 seconds | 13 | The opening frame and line make a promise a viewer can repeat back |
| Pacing and take picks | 18 | Beats land at the right energy, best takes chosen, no duplicate gestures |
| Story arc and distinctness | 9 | The set has a shape and does not resemble the night's other sets |
| Voiceover script, spoken not written | 13 | Sounds like a voice note to a friend; no stock phrases, no poem voice |
| Voice clone delivery | 9 | Natural pacing, no swallowed words, lines land on their scenes |
| Music and mix | 9 | Tone match, freshness, adds something; levels right; starts at frame one |
| Framing variety and polish | 9 | Shot sizes vary with intent; text placed and sized correctly |
| Technical clean | 10 | Export verified: frame count, loudness, face clearance, no defects |

For videos without voiceover, the two voice criteria drop out and the total is scaled to 100, stated plainly in the score line.

Speed, 10 points: computed from logged times, never judged by eye, and measured against the size of the job (clips handled, minutes of footage scouted, beats in the final cut, voice blocks, export passes). Only the editing phase earns speed points; file transfer time is infrastructure and is logged separately. Speed points count only if quality reaches about 80% of its marks, so rushing never pays.

## Pass mark and the moving goalpost

- Pass mark: 80 of 100.
- When any set scores 100, or the rolling 7 day v2 average reaches 95, tighten the rubric within a day: raise the pass mark, demand more inside existing criteria, promote proven experiment criteria into the main rubric, tighten the speed bands.
- Record every rubric change in the system file with its version and date. Scores in the run log always name the rubric version they were scored under.

## What the reviewer must always check, whatever the rubric says

- Every factual word in on-screen text is true of the footage.
- Text verified on the export, not the timeline: size, placement, readability against the real background, face clearance, safe zones on both Instagram and TikTok.
- Music justified in one line against the three tests.
- The hook's promise is visibly kept by the cut, including a payoff in the final beat if the hook asks for one.
- No known headline defects are being passed down the line for the creator to find.
