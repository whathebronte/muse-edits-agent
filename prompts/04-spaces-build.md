# Prompt: build the Invites and Brand Collabs spaces

Paste this to your Muse after the design system is in place (see the design prompt in the repo README, or `../DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`). It builds both tracker spaces, empty, from the templates in `../spaces/`.

---

Build me two spaces from this repo: https://github.com/whathebronte/muse-edits-agent

Read DESIGN-SYSTEM.md in full first, then spaces/invites/TEMPLATE.md and spaces/brand-collabs/TEMPLATE.md. Build me an Invites space and a Brand Collabs space that follow those templates almost exactly: same layout, same card faces, same statuses and colours, same behaviours, in the design language of DESIGN-SYSTEM.md.

Rules:
- Both spaces start empty. Never invent records, contacts or sample data for me; at most, clearly fake placeholders that I approve first.
- Before building the refresh, ask me: which mailbox or mailboxes to scan, my timezone, and (for Brand Collabs) my main currency and any others my fees arrive in. If I do not want a mailbox connected, build them for manual entry instead.
- The behaviours in the templates are the point, not decoration: a status or stage I set is never overwritten by a refresh, hidden items stay hidden across refreshes, fees are stored exactly as stated and never inferred, duplicates merge instead of piling up.
- Start my UI learnings doc from templates/UI-LEARNINGS.md if it does not exist yet, and keep it updated with my feedback on these spaces.
- Verify each space at phone width exactly as DESIGN-SYSTEM.md specifies, checking collapsed and expanded cards, before you call either done.
- Show me each finished space once, with a short summary of what is in it and what my refresh will do.
