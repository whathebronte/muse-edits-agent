# Track ledger

The single record of every music track use across ALL editing work: practice builds, real jobs, chat-driven builds, reels and stories alike. One line per use.

## The rules it serves

- **Check before every pick.** No ledger check, no pick. Every build reads this file before choosing music.
- **14 day cooldown.** A track used in any delivered piece is ineligible for another original build for 14 days from its last use, across all work. Exceptions, each logged on the line with its reason: (1) a benchmark recreation mirroring the audio of the creator's original post (fidelity is the point; logged as recreation-mirror, not a pick), (2) a track the creator names themself for that piece, (3) a brand or series requirement written in the brief. A pick inside cooldown without a logged exception is a reviewer fail.
- **Approval is per-use.** The creator approving a piece approves that use of the track, not the track in general. An approval never raises a track's selection priority; a recently approved track is less available, not more likely. When a fresh track graduates into the pool, the graduation records the use date and starts its cooldown.
- **Plans state the check.** Every set plan names its pick, the pick's source (pool, or fresh plus which source layer), and its last-used date from this ledger (or "never used"). The reviewer verifies the pick against this ledger as a checklist item.
- **Append on delivery.** Every build appends its uses here when the work is delivered.

## Format

One line per use:

`[date] | "[track]" | [artist] | [catalog id] | [what it was used in] | [source] | [creator's verdict, where given]`
